SAMPLE EDITORIAL CONCEPT / Memoir concept

Where the River Remembers

A fictional memoir concept about language, migration, and the complicated meaning of returning to the place that first shaped you.

Original illustrative concept. Not a published or represented client work.

THE EDITORIAL DIRECTION

In this illustrative concept, a narrator returns to a riverside town after years abroad. Familiar words feel different in the mouth; familiar streets contain unfamiliar lives. The return raises a question that cannot be answered by geography alone: what does it mean to come home?

The proposed narrative moves between childhood and the present, using the river as a point of continuity. Family meals, translated conversations, and small misunderstandings become ways to examine distance without reducing it to a simple story of loss or reconciliation.

This is an original sample concept, not a claim about a real author’s experience. Its editorial interest lies in the relationship between a particular voice and a carefully bounded personal narrative.
