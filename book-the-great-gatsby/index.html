The Great Gatsby

By F. Scott Fitzgerald

Nick Carraway enters Jay Gatsby’s world of wealth and longing, where an imagined future depends on recovering an elusive past.

A published literary reference. No representation or publishing relationship is claimed.

A READING PERSPECTIVE

Set around New York and Long Island, The Great Gatsby is narrated by Nick Carraway, whose proximity to Jay Gatsby draws him into a story of wealth, desire, and disillusionment.

Its compact form invites close attention to narrative distance, recurring images, and the gap between what people say and what they want. Gatsby’s aspirations are filtered through Nick’s account, making the act of telling part of the novel’s effect.
