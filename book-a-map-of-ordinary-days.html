SAMPLE EDITORIAL CONCEPT / Narrative nonfiction concept

A Map of Ordinary Days

An imagined exploration of the places we pass each day, and the overlooked rituals that turn a neighborhood into a home.

Original illustrative concept. Not a published or represented client work.

THE EDITORIAL DIRECTION

A bench outside a bakery. A footpath between two streets. The same window lit before dawn. This concept begins with an act of attention: looking closely at the ordinary places that structure a life.

Each imagined chapter pairs a small observation with a larger question about belonging. How do repeated journeys become memory? What do public places ask of the people who share them? How might noticing a place change our responsibility to it?

Presented as a sample nonfiction direction, the project demonstrates how a focused organizing idea can connect scene, reflection, and inquiry. It is not a published book or a represented client work.
