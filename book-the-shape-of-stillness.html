SAMPLE EDITORIAL CONCEPT / Literary fiction

The Shape of Stillness

Returning to the coastal house she inherited, a conservator discovers that the objects her mother kept tell a different story from the one she remembers.

Original illustrative concept. Not a published or represented client work.

THE EDITORIAL DIRECTION

After years restoring other people’s belongings, Mara returns to a house she has avoided since childhood. Its rooms are filled with carefully labeled boxes, unfinished letters, and a garden that has continued to grow without anyone’s permission.

As she prepares the property for sale, each object complicates her understanding of her mother. A neighbor’s recollection does not match a photograph. An ordinary receipt leads to an unfamiliar town. What begins as an inventory becomes a patient reckoning with memory, care, and the stories families leave unfinished.

This illustrative literary concept explores how material things hold emotional meaning. Its imagined editorial focus is an intimate voice, a measured pace, and a setting that changes as the narrator learns to see it differently.
