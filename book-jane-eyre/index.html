Jane Eyre

By Charlotte Brontë

A young governess tells her own story of hardship, attachment, and moral independence, insisting on the right to choose her future.

A published literary reference. No representation or publishing relationship is claimed.

A READING PERSPECTIVE

Charlotte Brontë’s novel follows Jane from childhood into her work as a governess at Thornfield Hall. Her attachment to Edward Rochester is tested by discoveries that force her to examine the terms on which she can belong.

Jane’s first-person narration makes voice central to the reading experience. Intimate address and reflection give a strong sense of a narrator who is not only remembering events but interpreting their place in her life.
