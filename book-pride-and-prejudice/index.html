Classic fiction / 1813

Pride and Prejudice

By Jane Austen

Elizabeth Bennet and Fitzwilliam Darcy encounter the limits of first impressions in a novel of family, class, and marriage.

A published literary reference. No representation or publishing relationship is claimed.

A READING PERSPECTIVE

Jane Austen’s novel follows Elizabeth Bennet as she negotiates family expectations and changes her understanding of Fitzwilliam Darcy. Its social world is shaped by marriage, money, and the stories people tell about one another.

For a reader interested in craft, the novel offers a study in dialogue and shifting perception. Exchanges that initially seem straightforward acquire new meaning as Elizabeth learns more about the people around her.
